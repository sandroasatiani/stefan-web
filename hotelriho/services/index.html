Our Services

We offer our guests to stay in one of our six rooms, equiped with comfortable double beds. We have a newly sanitary unit built next to the guest rooms with two shared hot showers and toilets and washing facility. 

 

We serve a full Svan breakfast including hot Chatshapuri and Sulguni cheese, lunch and dinner. We are quite flexible with the wishes of our guests and aside from serving the traditional Svan dishes like Kuptari (Beef in a breadroll) or pig specialities we are also experienced with vegitarian cooking. 

 

Aside from staying overnight and enjoying what our kitchen has to offer, we organise horse trips with local Georgian-, Russian- or English-speaking guides. We have up to 15 horses at our disposal and can offer trips to the bottom of Shchara Mountain with its 7 peaks raising up to 5860 m, up to the spring of Inguri river - or to Ailama Pass (3700 m) leading to the neighboring region of Lentekhi. ﻿You may also go horse riding on your own - what ever way you choose to enjoy our wonderful nature in Upper Svaneti.
